Norman Rockwell

Norman Rockwell was the most successful American illustrator of the 20th century. He was born February 3, 1894 and died November 8, 1978. He was a direct student of J.C. Leyendecker, and both their works even feature most prominently in the same place: the Saturday Evening Post. Rockwell is known to stick out from his contemporaries for the bold poliitical takes present in his public work.
