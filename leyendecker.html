J.C. Leyendecker

J.C Leyendecker was a German-American painter and illustrator in the early twentieth century who came to fame during the golden era of American illustration. He was born on March 23, 1874 and died July 25, 1951. He was most famous for his Saturday evening post covers. A large part of his work was inspired by his life as a homosexual man; however, this fact about him was not known to the public until long after his death. Rockwell was a student of his, and this can be seen in the obvious similarities in the art style.
